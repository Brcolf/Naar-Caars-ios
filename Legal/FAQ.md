# Frequently Asked Questions

## How do I join a community?

Naar's Cars is open: anyone may download the app and create an account. There are three ways into a community: enter an invite code someone in that community gave you, ask to join a public community (answering a couple of questions for its admins), or create your own community. Each community is a group of neighbors who help each other with rides and favors.

## How long does approval take?

Creating a community, or joining with a code for a community that admits people with a code, gets you in right away. Otherwise the community's owners and admins approve or decline your request, so how long it takes depends on the community. You can see the status of each request in the app while you wait.

## How do I request a ride or favor?

From the home screen, tap the + button to create a new ride or favor request. Add details like the date, time, pickup/dropoff location, and any notes. Your request will be visible to the members of the community you post it in.

## How do I claim a request?

Browse available requests on the dashboard or map view. When you find one you can help with, tap Claim to let the requester know you're available. You'll be connected via messaging to coordinate details.

## How do I send a message?

Open any conversation from the Messages tab. You can send text, photos (from camera or library), voice notes, and your current location using the + button in the message input bar.

## How do I report inappropriate content?

Tap and hold any message, post, or comment to see the report flag option. You can also report a person from their profile, and a community from its page. Select a reason and submit. Platform moderators review every report and take action as needed. Community admins do not see who reported. Platform moderators do.

## How do I block another user?

When reporting a message, you'll see an option to block the sender. Blocked users cannot message you. Additionally when viewing a user's public profile from requests (tapping on a user's avatar) you can block a user from the menu on the top right of the user's profile page.

## How do I delete my account?

Go to Profile → Settings → scroll to Delete Account. This permanently deletes your account and unlinks it with all associated data including rides, favors, messages, reviews, and posts. This action cannot be undone. If you are the only owner of a community, the app tells you first: ownership passes to the community's longest-standing admin, or, if it has no other admin, the community is closed and its members lose access.

## How do I change my notification preferences?

Go to Profile → Settings → Notifications to control which types of notifications you receive.

## How do I enable or disable biometric unlock?

Go to Profile → Settings and toggle Face ID / Touch ID on or off.

## Is my data shared or sold?

No. Naar's Cars does not sell your data, serve ads, or track you across apps. We collect only what's needed to make the app work. See our Privacy Policy for details.
