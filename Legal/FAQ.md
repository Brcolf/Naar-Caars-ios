# Frequently Asked Questions

## How do I join a community?

Naar's Cars is open: anyone may download the app and create an account. There are three ways into a community: enter an invite code someone in that community gave you, ask to join a public community (answering a couple of questions for its admins), or create your own community. Each community is a group of neighbors who help each other with rides and favors.

## How do I find and join communities?

Open the Explore tab (the magnifying glass at the right end of the tab bar). It lists the communities you belong to and the communities listed for anyone to find. Tap a community to open its page: you can see its name, description and member count, its upcoming open requests (without addresses), and its recent Town Hall posts before you join. The bar at the bottom of the page shows how it admits people: join right away, ask to join (the community's admins approve), or invite only (enter a code). You can also enter an invite code or start your own community from Explore. If you are browsing without an account, tap "Look around first" on the welcome screen to open Explore.

## How do I search for a community?

Open Explore and type in the search field at the top: a community's name, or a town or ZIP code. Results show listed communities by name and, for a place, the ones whose service area covers it, nearest first, with how each admits people. Tap one to open its page. You can search without an account.

## What does Use my location do?

In Explore, tap Use my location to find communities near you. The app reads your location once, only when you tap the button. Apple turns it into a town name, and the point (rounded to about 100 metres) is sent to our servers for that one search. It is not stored, and the app never reads it in the background. If you decline the permission, nothing is lost: type a town or ZIP code instead.

## How do I start a community that others can find?

Choose Start a community in Explore. Under Who can join, switch on Listed so anyone can find it, then set its service area: a place name and a radius, never an address. Before you finish, the app shows listed communities that already serve the area (the nearby check) so you can look at them first. Only the owner can list or unlist a community or change its area.

## What is the nearby check?

When you list a community, the app shows listed communities whose area overlaps yours. You can open them to see whether one already fits. If there is an overlap, you are asked to say why a new community is needed; platform admins see that reason with the new community.

## What can an owner switch on and off?

Owners control whether the community is listed, its service area and who may join, in Manage > Who can join and where. Members get one notice saying what changed. A community that is not listed does not appear in search or to guests; people join it with a code or link.

## Can two communities merge?

Yes, if both owners agree. In Manage, an owner chooses Merge with another community, finds it by name and proposes a merge with an optional note. The other owner can accept or decline within 7 days, and either owner can withdraw it. When it is accepted, the merged community's members, requests, posts and reports move to the surviving community, and the merged community's owner becomes a co-owner of the survivor. Everyone in both communities is told. A merge cannot be undone. A community can have more than one owner.

## What are example requests?

Test Community, the community anyone can look around in, shows example rides and favors and a few posts so you can see how the app works. They carry an Example label, nobody asked for them, and they cannot be claimed or answered.

## How long does approval take?

Creating a community, or joining with a code for a community that admits people with a code, gets you in right away. Otherwise the community's owners and admins approve or decline your request, so how long it takes depends on the community. You can see the status of each request in the app while you wait.

## How do I request a ride or favor?

From the home screen, tap the + button to create a new ride or favor request. Add details like the date, time, pickup/dropoff location, and any notes. Your request will be visible to the members of the community you post it in.

## How do I claim a request?

Browse available requests on the dashboard or map view. When you find one you can help with, tap Claim to let the requester know you're available. You'll be connected via messaging to coordinate details.

## How do I send a message?

Open any conversation from the Messages tab. You can send text, photos (from camera or library), voice notes, and your current location using the + button in the message input bar.

## How do I report inappropriate content?

Tap and hold any message, post, or comment to see the report flag option. You can also report a person from their profile, and a community from its page. Select a reason and submit. Community admins review reports about content in their community and can hide it; they never see who reported. Platform moderators review every report, do see who reported, and their decisions stand.

## How do I block another user?

When reporting a message, you'll see an option to block the sender. Blocked users cannot message you. Additionally when viewing a user's public profile from requests (tapping on a user's avatar) you can block a user from the menu on the top right of the user's profile page.

## How do I delete my account?

Go to Profile → Settings → scroll to Delete Account. This permanently deletes your account and unlinks it with all associated data including rides, favors, messages, reviews, and posts. This action cannot be undone. If you are the only owner of a community, the app tells you first: ownership passes to the community's longest-standing admin, or, if it has no other admin, the community is closed and its members lose access.

## How do I change my notification preferences?

Go to Profile → Settings → Notifications to control which types of notifications you receive.

## How do I enable or disable biometric unlock?

Go to Profile → Settings and toggle Face ID / Touch ID on or off.

## Is my data shared or sold?

No. Naar's Cars does not sell your data, serve ads, or track you across apps. We collect only what's needed to make the app work. See our Privacy Policy for details.
