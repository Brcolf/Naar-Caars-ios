## PR Impact Summary

1. **`CommunityServiceProtocol` changed.** `createCommunity(_ draft: NewCommunityDraft)` (→ `create_community_with_area`) replaced the four-argument call, and five methods were added across the two community protocols: `searchCommunities(name:)`, `findCommunitiesNear(_:)`, `checkCommunityArea(_:excluding:)` and `fetchServiceArea(communityId:)` on `CommunityServiceProtocol`, and `updateCommunityAccess(communityId:change:)` on `CommunityAdminServiceProtocol`. Every conformer is updated (`CommunityService`, `PreviewCommunityService`, the page preview's `RefusingPreview`, `StubCommunityService`, `StubCommunityAdminService`); a missed conformer would not compile.
2. **`CommunityMergeServiceProtocol` is new** (`searchMergeTargets`, `proposeMerge`, `fetchMergeProposal`, `respondToMerge`, `withdrawMerge`, `fetchMyCommunityMerges`), implemented by `CommunityService`, `PreviewCommunityService` and `StubCommunityService`, and injected into `CommunityContext`, `AdminPanelViewModel` and `CommunityMergeViewModel` (a nil default falls back to the community service only when it conforms, never to `.shared`, ruling I7).
3. **SwiftData trio for rides and favors:** `SDRide.isExample` / `SDFavor.isExample` (additive, default false), every `BackgroundSyncActor` insert, update and compare-before-write path, and `RequestFilterManager`'s reverse map. A missed compare would keep a stale flag until reinstall (`CommunityStoreScopingTests.testSyncAll_WritesAndRewritesIsExample`). The upgrade over an existing store kept the owner's session and data (gate O5/O6).
4. **`PushRefreshPlan`** refreshes the memberships and adds `.dashboard` on `community_event: "merged"` (fixture tests; seen in the gate: `get_my_memberships` on the foreground merged push, none on the proposal push). The registry is unchanged at 34; both new pushes are `announcement` rows.
5. **`CommunityContext`** swaps a merged-away community for its survivor in the Requests scope (`RequestsScope.afterMerges(_:)`) and falls back to All when the merge lookup fails (tests, including the sign-out-during-lookup case).
6. **One new `forceFullRefreshAndWait` trigger:** `(.dashboard, "mergedCommunity")`, from `CommunityMergeViewModel` after an accepted merge only.
7. **App Store checklist.** No new permission or SDK; the existing location purpose string gained the Explore clause (seen in the prompt, G4-16); the privacy policy, `Legal/PRIVACY-DISCLOSURES.md` and the App Privacy confirmation were drafted in Task 13 and wait for the owner; account deletion (unchanged code; its probe passes), reporting (community and Q&A), blocking and moderation are intact; the notification registry is unchanged. The review notes must say that example content is not real and that Test Community is the place to look around. **Installed builds:** the example rows are live, and a build from before Release 2 shows them to Test Community's members as real requests with no Example chip (I Can Help gives the generic error, Message reaches the NC Test Owner account); the owner decides whether to hide them until Release 2 reaches users ("Owed" item 5).
