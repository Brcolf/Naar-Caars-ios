## PR Impact Summary

1. **`CommunityServiceProtocol` changed.** `createCommunity(_ draft: NewCommunityDraft)` (→ `create_community_with_area`) replaced the four-argument call, and five methods were added across the two community protocols: `searchCommunities(name:)`, `findCommunitiesNear(_:)`, `checkCommunityArea(_:excluding:)` and `fetchServiceArea(communityId:)` on `CommunityServiceProtocol`, and `updateCommunityAccess(communityId:change:)` on `CommunityAdminServiceProtocol`. A missed compare would keep a stale flag until reinstall (`CommunityStoreScopingTests.testSyncAll_WritesAndRewritesIsExample`). The upgrade over an existing store kept the owner's session and data (gate O5/O6, and both devices on 2026-10-23).

6. **One new `forceFullRefreshAndWait` trigger:** `(.dashboard, "mergedCommunity")`, from `CommunityMergeViewModel` after an accepted merge only.
