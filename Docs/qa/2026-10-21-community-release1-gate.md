### Findings

- **F1 (low).** `CommunityPageViewModel.load()` decides member or pending mode from `CommunityContext.membership(for:)` and never refreshes the memberships, so a person whose request is approved while their pending page is open keeps "Waiting for approval", and pull-to-refresh does not change it, until they leave the page and return (Explore's load refreshes the memberships). The approval push (`membership_update`, approved) reached the simulator about three minutes late (its banner showed "3m ago" after a relaunch), so the foreground push path was not exercised in real time. Fix to consider: on pull-to-refresh of a pending or visitor page, refresh the memberships first.
- **F2 (unconfirmed).** Leaving from the page: no "left" toast in frames about 1 s and 5 s after confirming. `MyCommunitiesViewModel.leave` sets the toast after the dashboard refresh (≈0.7 s) and it lasts at least 2 s, so it may have shown between frames. Re-check with a screen recording.
- **F3 (plan-mandated, known).** At AX 5 the Requests header (subtitle, chips, three tiles) covers most of the screen (Task 9 deferred item).
- **F4 (owner call).** Find communities selects the Explore tab and shows whatever Explore last had on top (here the Test Public A page left open in O8), not the Explore root.
- Observation: the Town Hall push opens the post's comments sheet over the switched feed; that is the existing `townHallPostComments` route, unchanged in Release 1.

## PR Impact Summary

1. **`MainTabView` on the `Tab` API with Explore.** Four tabs (Requests, Messages, Town Hall, Profile) and Explore as the search-role tab. `MainTabView.onChange(of: selectedTab)` is still the single caller of `setVisibleDomain`: Requests → `.dashboard`, Messages → `.conversations`, Town Hall → `.townHall`, Profile and Explore → nil (`NavigationCoordinator.Tab.refreshDomain`). The Messages teardown stays keyed to leaving the Messages tab. No new coordinator trigger or call.
2. **`NavigationCoordinator.Tab` / `NavigationIntent` / `applyNotificationIntent`.** Community-admin routes (`.pendingMembers`, `.communityReports`, `.communityMembers`) and `.myCommunities`, `.joinCommunity` and `.enterApp` (authenticated) open on Explore (`MyCommunitiesView.exploreRoute(for:)`); platform-admin routes (`.pendingUsers`, `.adminReports`, `.adminPanel`) stay on Profile. New `NavigationIntent.explore` ("Find communities"). Every consumer was updated: `MainTabView`, `MyCommunitiesView`, `MyProfileView`, `CommunityTabView`, the routing tests. A missed consumer would have dropped a push on a tab with no handler. Verified on device (O6) warm and cold.
3. **`BadgeCountManager.BadgeCounts.explore`** (renamed from `profile`), read from `pending_members_total`. A missed reader would have shown the count on Profile. The badge shows on the search tab (O1, O3).
4. **`CommunityContext.requestsScope` gains `.excluding`**, changed only through `toggleRequestsInclusion(_:)` and persisted per account. A missed `switch` would not compile; a missed normaliser would leave an empty feed (tests in Task 8; on device O4, remembered across a relaunch).
5. **Server (`20261021_0001`, applied 2026-10-21 through the MCP after a rolled-back dry run, one block per call).** `my_visible_profile_ids()` also returns active members of listed (public, unarchived) communities, so `public_profiles` and the profile stats functions show them to every caller; new `is_listed_request(uuid, uuid)` (definer, anon and authenticated); the reviews SELECT policy gains an `is_listed_request` arm inside its approved gate; new `get_community_preview(uuid) → jsonb` (definer, `search_path=""`, anon and authenticated; listed and unarchived only, refusal code `not_listed`; at most 10 open requests and 5 posts with no address, note, description, gift, flight or cost key; banned posters and blocks either way left out). Probes `community_preview` and `listed_profiles` added and passing; the full probe file passes (G1). A Phase 5a build sees no difference except that `ProfileService.fetchReviews(forUserId:)` can return reviews of listed requests (already readable by guests).
6. **App Store checklist.** No new permission, SDK, entitlement, Info.plist key or privacy-manifest change. Data shown to signed-in non-members widened to what guests already see (members and requests of listed communities; privacy policy text in Task 11). Account deletion, reporting (now also "Report this community" on the community page, for visitors and members), blocking and moderation are intact (probes `account_deletion`, `reports`, `blocks`, `community_moderation`; the guest Report prompt and the visitor Report sheet on device). Notification delivery is unchanged (registry 34, no SQL CHECK change); routing changed tab only (item 2).
